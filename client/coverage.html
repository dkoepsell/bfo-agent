Coverage Coherence Layer

The wording contradicts itself.
A reasoner can prove it.

A structural audit of a real, complete, public-domain insurance policy — the NFIP Standard Flood Insurance Policy, Dwelling Form.

Coverage is not a score. A loss either triggers the indemnity obligation or it does not. Run a hypothetical loss through this policy's own wording and a reasoner returns one of two verdicts a number never could: this coverage cannot pay anything, or this loss is both covered and not covered — and it names the clauses responsible.

Every clause below is quoted verbatim from federal regulation (44 CFR Part 61, Appendix A(1)), which carries no copyright, so each finding is independently checkable against the source. These are structural facts about the wording, established by a logical reasoner — not opinions, and not corrections. What to do about them is the review.
