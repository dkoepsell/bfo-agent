A freezer is covered.
The sofa beside it is not.

Neither fact is asserted anywhere. Both are derived by an OWL 2 DL reasoner from two provisions of a public federal regulation. Below you can put your own facts to the same kernel and watch it decide.

A coverage kernel that answers three questions and refuses to guess at a fourth: is this loss covered, is it uncovered, is the wording contradictory, and if the exclusions were never closed, undetermined. Every verdict is derived, and a contradiction comes back with the axioms responsible rather than a shrug.

The demonstrator is the NFIP Standard Flood Insurance Policy, Dwelling Form, published at 44 CFR Part 61, Appendix A(1). It is a work of the United States federal government and carries no copyright, so every verdict here is independently checkable against the source.
